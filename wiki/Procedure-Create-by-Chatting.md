# Procedure 8: Create by Chatting

This procedure makes a picture and then a short video from it, by describing both in plain words in Chat.

**Before you start:** save your Replicate key under Image in Settings. Chat can run on the phone ([Procedure 7](Procedure-Install-a-Local-Model.md)) or through OpenRouter. In Settings, under Chat, **Create images and videos from chat** must be on. It is on at first.

**Cost:** the chat itself is free on the phone and a fraction of a cent through OpenRouter. Each job costs its model's normal price, which the job card shows before you tap Generate.

## Steps

1. On the main screen, tap **Chat**.
2. Ask for a picture in plain words, such as `make a picture of a red fox in the snow, wide`.
3. The reply carries a **job card**. Read its summary, such as "Image, wide", and the prompt the image model will receive.
4. Change any word of the prompt if you like. To use a different model, pick it from **Model**. The details line shows the likely cost.
5. Tap **Generate**. The finished picture appears under the plan, in the chat.
6. Ask for the next step, such as `turn that picture into a 5 second video where the fox walks away`.
7. The new job card says **Starts from the picture**, with the fox picture's prompt. Pick a video model. **WAN 2.2 fast** costs about 5 cents for a 480p clip, and **WAN 2.7** about 50 cents at 720p.
8. Tap **Generate**. The app uploads the picture as the first frame, and the clip appears under the plan when it is done.

## Tips

- "That picture" means the newest picture. To point at another one, tap **Talk about this** on it. A line above the message bar then says which picture the next message is about.
- To work on a photo from your phone, tap the photo button at the left of the message bar and pick the photo. Then say what to do with it, such as `make it black and white` or `turn it into a 5 second video`. The chat model cannot see the photo itself, so describe the change plainly.
- If the chat answers in words instead of making a plan, tap **Use my words** under the reply. A job card then uses your message exactly as you typed it. With a picture chosen, it can also edit that picture with your words.
- **Cancel** closes a plan without spending anything. Nothing runs until you tap Generate.
- A video plan that attached a picture you did not want has **Without the picture**.
- Edits work the same way: `make the last picture black and white` gives an edit card. [Procedure 9](Procedure-Edit-an-Image.md) explains editing in more detail.
